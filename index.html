Founder's Story

Built by Peace Siah, Founder of Destibeau

"Skill builds expertise.
Perspective determines how far you go."

In 2010, as a full-time housewife facing her lowest point, Peace took a government loan and taught herself beauty from scratch. By 2011 she was serving clients door-to-door. On 11 November 2013 she opened the first Peace Beauty — and never looked back. Thirteen years later, Destibeau has shaped the confidence of over 9,450 women and men across Seremban.
